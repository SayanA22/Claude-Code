## Name
Scam Spotter

## Summary (max 132 characters)
Paste a suspicious message or link and get a plain-English answer. Everything stays on your computer.

## Category
Productivity (or "Accessibility" if offered). Language: English.

## Description

Not sure if a text, email, or link is a scam? Scam Spotter gives you a quick second opinion in big, plain English.

HOW IT WORKS
• Right-click a link or highlighted text and choose "Is this a scam?"
• Or click the Scam Spotter icon, paste the message, and press Check.

YOU GET A SIMPLE ANSWER
🔴 Likely scam
🟡 Be careful
🟢 Looks okay

Every answer lists exactly why, one short sentence per warning sign, such as:
• A website pretending to be a real company (like amaz0n.com)
• Requests for gift cards, crypto, or wire transfers
• Fake virus warnings with a phone number
• Prize or lottery claims
• Government impersonation (IRS, Social Security, Medicare)
• Requests for passwords or verification codes
• Pressure to keep it a secret or act right now
• Fake delivery fees and refund tricks

Every answer ends with the same advice: when in doubt, ask a family member before clicking or paying.

BUILT FOR EVERYONE
• Big text, high contrast, and large buttons
• Warning signs are highlighted inside your message, with numbered reasons that match
• Light and dark looks, using your computer's setting
• No jargon

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
• Nothing leaves your computer. No tracking, no accounts, no network requests
• Only one permission: adding the right-click menu item
• It cannot read the pages you visit

IMPORTANT
Scam Spotter checks for common warning signs. It is a second opinion, not a guarantee, and we never say something is "safe". When in doubt, ask someone you trust.

## Single purpose (the store asks for this)
Check a message or link that the user pastes or right-clicks for common scam warning signs, and explain the result in plain English.

## Permission justification
**contextMenus**: Adds the "Is this a scam?" item to the right-click menu for links and selected text. No other permissions are requested.
