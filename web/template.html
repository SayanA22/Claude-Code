What it works out

Offers change the price with quantity. "3 for $10" is only the cheapest at multiples of three, and if that is the only price shown you cannot buy one at a third of it. The answer says how many you must take.

You pay for packs, not for grams. Ask for the amount you need and the ranking switches to the cheapest way to actually get it — which is often not the lowest price per unit.

A US fluid ounce is 29.57 ml; an imperial one is 28.41 ml, and the pints differ by 20%. The currency picks the right table, or set it above.
