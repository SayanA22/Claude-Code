# Scam Spotter Privacy Policy

*Last updated: November 2026*

**Scam Spotter does not collect, store, or send any information about you. Everything happens on your own computer.**

## What the extension does with your information

- When you paste text, highlight text, or right-click a link and choose "Is this a scam?", the extension checks it using rules that are built into the extension itself.
- The text you check is **never sent anywhere**. The extension makes **no network requests** at all.
- Nothing you check is saved. When you close the window, it is gone.

## What the extension does NOT do

- It does not collect personal information.
- It does not use analytics, tracking, cookies, or advertising.
- It does not read the pages you visit. It has no permission to do so.
- It does not use accounts or sign-ins.
- It does not sell or share data, because it has no data.

## Permissions

Scam Spotter asks for one permission only:

- **contextMenus**: to add "Is this a scam?" to the right-click menu.

It has no host permissions and no content scripts.

## Limits of the tool

Scam Spotter gives a second opinion based on common warning signs. It can be wrong. When in doubt, ask a family member before clicking or paying.

## Changes

If this policy changes, the new version will be posted here with a new date.
