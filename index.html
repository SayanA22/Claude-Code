Install as an App

Add Mile Trainer to your home screen for an app-like, offline-capable experience.

iPhone (Safari): tap the Share icon, then "Add to Home Screen".
Android (Chrome): tap the menu (⋮), then "Add to Home screen" / "Install app".

Workout Reminders (Push Notifications)

Get notified about your scheduled workout. This requires a small notification server (included in this project's server/ folder) — deploy it once, then point this app at its URL below. On iPhone, install this app to your Home Screen first (iOS 16.4+) before enabling notifications.
