Prices

        
Every coded line is divided by the Medicare national rate for that code. Private plans pay roughly 2.5× Medicare on average, so anything above 4× is surfaced with the discount to ask for — and the hospital's own cash price, which it is required to publish, as the lever.

      

      

        
Federal rights

        
The engine applies the right law for the patient. Insured with an out-of-network ER clinician: the No Surprises Act bans the balance bill. Uninsured and billed more than $400 over the good faith estimate: the federal dispute process is open for 120 days, for a $25 fee.

      

      

        
Then it writes the letter

        
Each finding becomes a numbered objection with the rule it violates, addressed to the billing office, with a records request and a hold on collections. Print it, sign it, send it.

      

    

    

      Read this part. Itemize is a working prototype built for the Blue Ocean Student Entrepreneur Competition. It is not legal, medical, or financial advice, and it does not replace a licensed patient advocate or attorney. The Medicare reference rates below the hood are rounded national averages used for benchmarking only; a production version reads live CMS fee schedules and the hospital's own machine-readable price file, both of which are public. Nothing you type here leaves your browser — there is no server, no account, and no bill data stored anywhere.
    

  





  

    
Paste your itemized bill

    
One line item per row. The parser looks for a CPT/HCPCS code, a description, units, and a dollar amount in any order — commas, pipes, or spacing all work.
