# Blue Ocean Student Entrepreneur Competition — rules and submission checklist

Everything here was taken from the competition's own pages (`blueoceancompetition.org`)
while preparing this entry. **Re-check the site before you submit** — dates roll every
cycle and a rule violation is an automatic disqualification.

## Judging criteria — five categories, 20% each

Each is scored 5 (excellent) down to 1 (poor). This is the entire scoring surface, so the
pitch should visibly hit all five.

| Criterion | What they are asking | Where this entry answers it |
|---|---|---|
| **Value innovation** | A novel idea that achieves differentiation **and** low cost at the same time | ERRC grid: contingency fee and intake eliminated; legal coverage and evidence raised. Free audit with near-zero marginal cost. |
| **Market potential** | Is the problem significant, with broad appeal | 100M+ US adults with medical debt; $220B owed; the three tiers of noncustomers |
| **Commercial viability & scalability** | A business model that supports mass adoption | Free core; certified dispatch, dispute filing, employer licensing. Client-side engine = flat serving cost |
| **Technical feasibility** | The technology exists or is plausibly buildable | A working prototype, plus every data input being free and public (CMS + 45 CFR 180 files) |
| **Presentation & blue ocean tools** | Clear articulation using the frameworks | Strategy canvas, three tiers of noncustomers, ERRC grid, buyer utility map — all in the strategy pack |

The competition specifically expects the **strategy canvas**, the **three tiers of
noncustomers**, and the **eliminate-reduce-raise-create grid**. The **buyer utility map**
is also named in their guidance on what makes a winning pitch. All four are built.

## Hard rules

- [ ] Video pitch **under 5 minutes**, at **normal playback speed** (no speeding up to fit)
- [ ] Video file **under 1 GB**
- [ ] **English** only
- [ ] Uploaded to **YouTube** with the hashtag **#blueoceancompetition**, and the link submitted
      on the official form (some cycles also ask for the file itself)
- [ ] **Original work** — not copied from anywhere
- [ ] **One entry per team**; all team member names entered on the submission form
- [ ] Every participant registered individually, solo or on a team
- [ ] Team of **1 to 5** students, high-school age (roughly 14–18), any school or mix of schools
- [ ] The online **Blue Ocean mini-course** completed before submitting
- [ ] Registration and submission both in **before the deadline** (a February date; confirm the
      current cycle's on the site)

A working prototype is **not required** — mock-ups are explicitly allowed — but the
competition's own FAQ says prototypes improve your chances. This entry has one, so lead with it.

## Before you hit submit

- [ ] Watch the video start to finish with a stopwatch. Under 5:00, with room to spare.
- [ ] Bill text in the demo is legible after YouTube compression (record at 1280×800+).
- [ ] Audio is clean — bad audio costs more points than bad video.
- [ ] The prototype link works in a private browsing window.
- [ ] Every statistic you say aloud traces to a source in `docs/research-and-sources.md`.
- [ ] The disclaimer is visible on screen at least once (this is a bill-audit tool; do not
      let it read as legal advice).
- [ ] Names spelled correctly on the form. This is not the place to lose points.
