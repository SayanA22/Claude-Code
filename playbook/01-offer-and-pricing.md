# What you sell, and for how much

## The ladder

Sell the cheap thing first. Each rung makes the next one easier.

### 1. The free sample report — $0
Generated with `--no-quote`. This is marketing, not work. Never spend more than
15 minutes on one. Its only job is to earn a reply.

### 2. Full audit — $500 – $2,500
The complete scan across the whole site, plus a manual review pass, plus a
prioritised remediation roadmap and a walkthrough call.

| Site size | Price |
|---|---|
| Under 10 pages (local business) | $500 – $900 |
| 10–50 pages (established SMB) | $900 – $1,800 |
| 50+ pages, or a public entity | $1,800 – $2,500 |
| Web app with authenticated flows | $2,500+ |

Price on **site complexity**, never on hours. The moment you quote hourly you
are being paid for typing rather than for the risk you remove.

### 3. Remediation — $2,000 – $15,000
You fix what the audit found. Quote from the audit, with a fixed price and a
defined scope. This is the largest single cheque and it is far easier to sell
to someone who already bought an audit from you.

### 4. Monitoring retainer — $150 – $600 / month
Monthly re-scan, a short report, and a fix budget of a few hours. Cheap for
them, near-automatic for you, and the reason this business is worth building
rather than just doing once.

> Sites regress. Someone adds an unlabelled form or a low-contrast banner and
> the score slides. That is not a flaw in your work — it is precisely why the
> retainer exists, and you should say so plainly when you sell it.

### 5. Training / policy — $1,500 – $5,000
A half-day session for their web team, plus an accessibility statement and an
internal checklist. Good for public entities, which frequently need to show
process rather than just a fixed site.

## Packages that close

Most buyers do not want a menu. Give them three options and let them pick the middle.

**Starter — $1,200**
Full audit, prioritised roadmap, one walkthrough call. Good for a small
business that wants to know where it stands.

**Fix It — $3,500** ← *most people should buy this*
Everything in Starter, plus remediation of all critical and serious issues,
plus a verification re-scan proving each fix holds.

**Covered — $3,500 + $350/month**
Everything in Fix It, plus monthly monitoring, a quarterly manual review, and
an accessibility statement for their site.

Adjust the absolute numbers to your market. Keep the **structure**: three
options, the middle one obviously correct, the third adding recurring revenue.

## Setting your hourly rate in the tool

`--rate` drives the generated estimate. It is not what you charge the client —
it is the input to a range. Guidance:

- Just starting, no portfolio: `--rate 100`
- Some experience, can show past work: `--rate 150`
- Specialist positioning or public-sector work: `--rate 200+`

The estimate already applies a 1.35× multiplier for QA, retesting, and
coordination. Do not add another buffer on top and then wonder why you lost
the deal on price.

## Rules that will save you money

1. **Never quote on the call.** "I'll send scope and pricing today" buys you
   the time to actually read the findings.
2. **Deposit up front.** 50% to start, 50% on delivery. No exceptions for a
   first client, however friendly they seem.
3. **Scope in writing, including what is excluded.** Third-party embeds you do
   not control — booking widgets, maps, chat, payment iframes — are the single
   most common source of a job going over. Exclude them explicitly, in writing,
   and offer to raise them with the vendor as a separate line item.
4. **Price the risk removed, not the hours spent.** A form fix that takes forty
   minutes may be the thing that ends their exposure. That is not a
   forty-minute problem.
5. **Walk away from bad fits.** A prospect who wants a "compliance guarantee"
   or an overlay widget is a refund and a bad review waiting to happen.
