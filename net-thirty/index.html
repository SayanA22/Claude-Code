The read

What each client really pays

Solid bar is what you cleared per hour of your life spent on them — every hour, billable or not. The hollow bar behind it is the rate on the contract.

Client by client

Ordered worst first. The move is the one thing to do about each before the next invoice goes out.

Money you are owed

Everything invoiced and not yet in the bank, aged from its due date.

Log time

Log the unbillable hours too — the status calls, the fourth round of revisions, the proposal you wrote for free. Those are the hours that decide your real rate.

Invoices

Issue date and paid date are what the payment-lag read is built from. Leave paid blank until the money lands.

Clients & projects

A project is either hourly or a fixed fee. A monthly retainer is a fixed fee with the hours you promised — that is how the overrun becomes visible.

Settings

Your floor rate is the number below which the work is not worth taking — overheads, unpaid time and time off already priced in. Everything on the Read is measured against it.
